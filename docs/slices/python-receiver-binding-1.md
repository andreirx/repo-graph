### 2.2 Evidence taxonomy (each state and its consumer)

- **Python receiver.**
  - A carrier `self`/`cls` call has three outcomes:
    - it hits in the hierarchy: static, unchanged;
    - it hits an MRO collision: unresolved `self_call_ambiguous_mro`, unchanged;
    - it misses: unresolved `self_call_hierarchy_miss` with its pool, new.
  - A carrier `self`/`cls` call whose receiver is a proven parameter is decided by its class hierarchy alone. A file-level namespace import whose alias is `self` or `cls` is never consulted for it (new, R1 precedence): a hit is static, a miss is unresolved `self_call_hierarchy_miss` with its pool, and the aliased module's same-named function is at most a candidate in that pool.
  - A carrier `self`/`cls` call without the key whose edge `extractor` is exactly `python-core:0.1.0` keeps HEAD's stage order: the namespace stage binds it `static` as at HEAD; otherwise the hierarchy decides, a hit static and a miss unresolved `self_call_hierarchy_miss` with its pool (new: the miss no longer falls through to a bare-name binding).
  - A carrier `self`/`cls` call whose receiver is unproven or unreadable, or whose missing key comes from any other writer, is decided by neither stage. It is unresolved `self_call_receiver_unproven` with every candidate (new, D-PRB-CARRIER-1).
  - A `self`/`cls` call with an absent or malformed carrier first meets the namespace stage as today. A file-level alias of that name binds it `static` there, unchanged. Otherwise it is unresolved `self_call_without_class_context` with its pool (new). It never binds by name.
  - Any other receiver with a unique candidate is inferred, with a pool of one recorded.
  - Any other receiver with two or more candidates is unresolved with every candidate and `nameOnlyReason: ambiguous_name`. Its verdict is today's.
  - Any other receiver with no candidate is unresolved exactly as today.
- **Self-call carrier `receiverBinding`** (the extractor's lexical fact; one reader, three evidence states, the absent state split by the edge's writer):
  - Absent, writer exactly `python-core:0.1.0` (every pre-slice snapshot; copied forward and re-resolved by a refresh): HEAD's stage order with a terminal hierarchy miss (state iii-a); TOOLCHAIN-STALENESS-1's Python line marks the snapshot until `rmap repo rebuild <path>`.
  - Absent, writer `python-core:0.2.0` (which always writes the key) or unrecognized: unproven (state iii-b), unresolved `self_call_receiver_unproven` with the pool.
  - Present-valid: `"parameter"` gives the hierarchy stage (state i). `"unproven"` gives unresolved `self_call_receiver_unproven` with the pool (state ii).
  - Present-malformed (a non-string or an unknown string): unreadable, treated as unproven (state iv). It is never read as `"parameter"`, never an error, never a dropped row.
  - A carrier whose class context itself is absent or malformed is R1b's, whatever its `receiverBinding`.
- **Edge `resolution`.**
  - `static`/`dynamic` are certain: they form the default sets and every certain tally, and they are the rate's numerator.
  - `inferred` is the remainder. It is included by the flag on `callers`/`callees`, listed under `--full` in explain, counted everywhere a remainder is stated, and kept in the rate's denominator.
  - A compiler-promoted row is `static` from `compiler-promotion:0.2.0` on. A pre-slice `compiler-promotion:0.1.0` row stays `inferred` until re-enriched.
- **Inferred edge `metadata_json`** (the name-only contract; one decoder, three states):
  - Present-valid, `{basis, receiver, candidates}`: served and rendered by its basis.
  - Absent (NULL or empty) or present-malformed (any other shape, e.g. the legacy promoter's `{promotedFrom, receiverType, methodName}`): served with `basis: unrecorded` and the row's `extractor`. It is rendered `inferred: compiler-resolved call recorded by an older rmap — run rmap repo rebuild <path>` only when the extractor is exactly `compiler-promotion:0.1.0` (the verified legacy writer). Any other writer or version renders `inferred: basis unknown — investigate`, with the extractor kept in JSON.
  - None of the three is an error, a drop, or the name-only label.
- **Unresolved pool `metadata_json`** (`nameOnlyCandidates` plus `nameOnlyReason`):
  - A valid carrier with reason `self_call_hierarchy_miss` classifies as that basis.
  - No valid carrier with reason `self_call_without_class_context` classifies as that basis.
  - A valid carrier with reason `self_call_receiver_unproven` classifies as that basis.
  - Reason `ambiguous_name` keeps the row's existing verdict.
  - A malformed array or an unknown reason falls through to today's rules (the `mroCandidates` precedent). The `sites` view shows it as `candidate_reason: unreadable`.
- **Remainder payloads** (`inferred`, `unresolved_naming`, `unresolved_from`, `inferred_count`, `inferred_edges_on_route` with its `search_depth`, `inferred_calls`). Absent means none, or an older daemon, and renders exactly as today; present means a count ≥ 1 with its `by_basis`. Trust always measures `inferred_calls`, from the persisted key or the live count; the field is serialized only when positive. `inferred_edges_on_route` without `search_depth` is a decode error in rgr, never a guessed bound.
- **Trust's two figures.** A persisted aggregate WITH its `inferred_calls` key is served as persisted. With either missing, both figures come from the live certainty split, never one persisted and one live.

### 2.3 Placement and the size guardrail

**Placement of policy and mechanism.**
- Policy (which receivers count as evidence; what a hierarchy miss is) lives in the resolver beside the stages it extends. That is `resolver.rs`'s existing responsibility, not a new one.
- The receiver's lexical binding is an extraction FACT. It is read where the syntax tree exists, in the Python extractor, and stored on the carrier the extractor already stamps. The resolver has no syntax tree, so it could not recover the fact. `extractor.rs` is 2,514 lines. The receiver facts extend the carrier's existing responsibility, which is stamping the self-call's binding evidence, beside `current_class`. They add no new responsibility to the file (CLAUDE.md's guardrail), and D-PRB-CARRIER-1 names the file.
- The basis vocabulary lives where every other basis lives (classification).
- The rate's operand lives in the ONE projection (`CallReliabilityView`) that every rate surface already consumes.

**Introduced abstractions.**
- `storage::call_remainder_reads` reads what the certain call sets leave out for one symbol (counts and the bounded `sites` list), and holds the one inferred-row evidence decoder (`inferred_call_evidence`) that the remainder reads and `find_direct_callers`/`find_direct_callees` share.
