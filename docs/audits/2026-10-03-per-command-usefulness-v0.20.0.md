## Verdict
The queue did what it was cut to do and the reviewers agree where it matters: all nine measurable slices deliver their recorded literal in the field, nothing regressed, no command crashes, and HONESTY moved a step because the aggregate surfaces now state every remainder. HIT did not move: the gains (poco docs, django callers, FRAKTAG's workspace edge) are offset by what a user meets one step later — `0 imports` on first-party files whose aliases the index cannot bind, kafka's sixty empty declared sets, a repo-wide Summary under a module heading. Scope misattribution is dead at every site it was named on and alive on two it was not; its successor, the silent per-file remainder, lives exactly on the surfaces an agent reaches after `orient` and `trust` have pointed it at one file or module.
