Never worked (the required field predates every slice; no corpus had an unreadable admitted doc until the widened rule). Requirements violated in the field: RG-REQ-008-L05, L08. Smallest fix: `Option<String>` with `#[serde(default)]`, renderer and `filtered_json_view` re-emit only when present, a fixture with an absent hash on an unreadable-counted entry, a poco live proof (`+1 unreadable, counted`); regression watch: every other corpus byte-identical.
