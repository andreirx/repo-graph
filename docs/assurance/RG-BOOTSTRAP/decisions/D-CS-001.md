# D-CS-001 — Should CS-C12 and CS-C13 be amended from directory-wide absence assertions to assertions over the persisted exclusion predicate?

Raised: 2026-09-20 by the COMPLEXITY-SCOPE-1 implementation review (review-COMPLEXITY-SCOPE-1-0 under INPUT-1, codex gpt-5.6-terra), result decision-required; blocking reason: "The approved allocation requires all sixteen checks to pass, yet two mandatory checks currently fail because their oracle contradicts the governing requirement. Resolved: 2026-09-20 by the in-place manager (operator), option 1, under docs/MANAGER.md § Oracle corrections (human decision 2026-09-14): the change alters only two checks' negative-grep literals (no allocation set, check-id set, requirement text or candidate path); recorded as OC-1 and OC-2 in docs/assurance/COMPLEXITY-SCOPE-1/oracle-corrections.md and carried as INPUT-2 (document item PREP-2, independent review). The human may override.

## Problem

CS-C12's and CS-C13's full-section sub-assertions required that no `--full` complexity row survive under `codegraph-kernel/grammars/` (CS-C12) or `/testsuite/` (CS-C13). RG-REQ-009-L01 defines exclusion by PERSISTED FACTS — `files.is_generated` (a first-lines marker), `files.is_test` (the structural detector), the vendored path-segment rule — and states "a file distinguishable by no persisted fact stays ranked". Two hand-written classes live under those directories and carry no fact: tree-sitter EXTERNAL SCANNERS (`codegraph-kernel/grammars/scala/scanner.c` begins `#include "tree_sitter/alloc.h"`; `tree_sitter_scala_external_scanner_scan` at :295, cx 83; `kotlin/scanner.c scan_automatic_semicolon`, cx 38) and poco's CppUnit test sources (788 files under `*/testsuite/*`, `is_test = 0` on the before AND after stores — the shipped detector recognises gtest markers only, RG-REQ-001-L07, which this slice preserves; `Foundation/testsuite/src/TestApp.cpp:240 int main(int argc, char** argv)`, cx 42). The first admission's candidate passed every other assertion of both checks (the five codegraph and two poco generated files, the new headlines, `--include-all`, the docs movement, the store-derived seam) and 14 of the 16 checks; the two oracles were wrong, the product was right (the oracle was written by the manager from the 2026-09-12 spec's path-level expectations without checking the stores for the two directories — a packet defect).

## Options (as the reviewer put them)

1. Amend the two checks to predicate-based assertions — reward: verification aligned with RG-REQ-009-L01 (generated, vendored and structurally-test files excluded; files with no persisted fact remain visible); preserves the frozen marker list, the unchanged `is_test` detector and the no-fabricated-exclusion rule; retains the generated-file, headline, JSON-seam and include-all checks and replaces only the over-broad path greps; risk: the allocation text changes and needs its authorized amendment/review; hand-written scanners and unsupported CppUnit tests remain visible as honest residuals. RECOMMENDED by the reviewer.
2. Require whole-directory exclusion — reward: the present negative greps pass without changing their oracle; risk: needs a new path-based generated/test classification rule or changed `is_test` semantics, hides hand-written code, contradicts RG-REQ-009-L01 and the VISION's honest current-state facts; violates the frozen-marker and unchanged-is_test boundaries; cannot be applied within this slice.

## Resolution — option 1, as OC-1 and OC-2 with re-review

CS-C12's negative grep becomes `'grammars/[a-z_]+/parser\.c|__tests__/'` (the generated parsers by name; every codegraph `__tests__/` row above threshold carries `is_test = 1` on the fresh store); CS-C13's becomes `'dependencies/|bison_parser'`. Both residual classes are named in the check inputs and §8 (follow-up IS-TEST-CPPUNIT-1: a CppUnit marker rule for the structural detector — not this slice). The preserved candidate (.agent-manager/slices/COMPLEXITY-SCOPE-1.superseded-INPUT-1/candidate-1.patch) is re-applied at the second admission together with the fix for the review's other finding F-CS-001 (a stale doc comment in `agent/src/aggregators/complexity.rs` says `count_high_complexity_symbols` is "left alone" while the aggregator no longer reads it — a code-comment defect, corrected in the candidate, not in the oracle).
